       ▼

Identified Discrepancies & Disconnections:
1. Disconnected Islands:
   - The Customer domain (customers, customer_addresses) and the Catalog domain (categories, products, product_images) are completely isolated from each other.
   - There are no bridge tables (orders, order_items, carts, cart_items) implemented in SQL to link customers with products.

2. Deletion Action Risks:
   - customers -> auth.users uses ON DELETE RESTRICT: If a user is deleted from auth.users, deletion fails if a customer record exists.
   - products -> categories uses ON DELETE RESTRICT: Categories containing products cannot be deleted unless products are reassigned or removed first.

3. Customer PK Naming:
   - Table customers uses user_id as its PK, while customer_addresses uses customer_id as the referencing FK column. This is functionally valid but requires careful mapping in JPA (@JoinColumn(name = "customer_id", referencedColumnName = "user_id")).

6. CONFIRMED BUSINESS RULES (FROM PROJECT ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sources: 08_VARELLION_BACKEND_DATABASE_SECURITY_SPEC.md, 06_VARELLION_INITIAL_CATALOG_AND_SITE_DATA.md, application.properties

1. Commercial Model:
   - Primary launch mode: Guest Checkout + Cash on Delivery (COD) + WhatsApp handoff coordination.
   - Orders must support guests who do NOT possess an account in auth.users or public.customers.
   - Consequently, in any future orders table, customer_id MUST be NULLABLE with ON DELETE SET NULL.
   - Customer identity snapshot (customer_name, customer_phone) and shipping snapshot (governorate, city_area, detailed_address) MUST be stored directly on the order record, never relying on a foreign key to customer_addresses.

2. Monetary Rules & Currency:
   - Standard currency: EGP (Egyptian Pound).
   - Numerical precision: NUMERIC(10, 2) exact decimal, non-negative CHECK bounds.
   - Subtotal must be calculated server-side; client pricing is never authoritative.

3. Shipping Fee & Pending Totals:
   - If shipping rate for a selected governorate is not configured, the order is accepted with shipping_fee = NULL, shipping_fee_status = 'PENDING', and total_amount = NULL.
   - Final total is confirmed by administration via WhatsApp before fulfilment.

4. Stock Reservation & 24-Hour Expiration:
   - Default reservation window: 24 hours (configured in application.properties: varellion.store.reservation-hours=24).
   - Timestamp confirmation_deadline generated server-side at order creation.
   - Stock reservation must be atomic; reservations transition from ACTIVE -> CONSUMED (on confirmation) or RELEASED (on expiration/cancellation).
   - Expiration and cancellation must share an idempotent release mechanism to prevent double stock restoration.

5. Product Variants & Attributes:
   - Colors and sizes are treated as separate standalone products or sellable variants with their own SKUs, never virtual options that share unreserved stock.

6. Historical Snapshot Integrity:
   - order_items must capture product_name_ar, product_name_en, sku, unit_price, quantity, and line_total as immutable snapshots to prevent subsequent catalog edits from altering historical receipts.
   - product_id in order_items must use ON DELETE RESTRICT (or SET NULL) to prevent catalog purges from cascading into sales history.
