# VARELLION Accessories — Marketing Content Guide

> **Document ID:** `07_VARELLION_MARKETING_CONTENT_GUIDE.md`  
> **Purpose:** Optional guide for planning social-media content for VARELLION.  
> **Scope:** Editorial approach and channel formats only; this document is not a source of product facts, prices, offers, stock, or customer claims.  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-10

---

## 1. Scope and Source Rules

This guide covers marketing content planning for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. Use it only when creating social-media plans, scripts, captions, or content calendars.

The business and product truth rules in `03_VARELLION_BUSINESS_PRODUCT_CONTENT_SPEC.md` apply to all marketing channels. Actual product information, pricing, active offers, profile links, and contact details must come from `06_VARELLION_INITIAL_CATALOG_AND_SITE_DATA.md` or another owner-approved source.

Do not treat an example format or content idea in this guide as evidence that a product, discount, bundle, or social-media account exists.

## 2. Marketing Objectives

Content may support:

1. Brand awareness
2. Product discovery
3. Product education
4. Comparison and informed consideration
5. Visits to an approved product or category page
6. Customer questions and approved contact flow
7. Post-purchase education when product instructions are available

Do not optimize for engagement at the expense of accuracy, trust, or platform-appropriate communication.

## 3. Content Pillars

Possible editorial pillars:

- Product showcase using approved real assets
- Technology tips backed by reliable information
- Buying guides based on verified comparison criteria
- Product education and usage guidance
- Approved deals and offers
- Brand story using confirmed company facts
- Setup inspiration using real or clearly illustrative assets
- Problem/solution education without unsupported performance claims
- Approved new-arrival announcements
- Customer stories only with authentic content and permission

## 4. Content Formats

Potential formats include short videos, demonstrations, educational posts, comparisons, carousels, buying tips, unboxings, product announcements, offer announcements, and behind-the-scenes content.

Every post that names a product or promotion must be checked against current approved data before publication. Do not manufacture a customer story, test result, product demonstration, discount, specification, certification, or stock message.

## 5. Instagram Guidance

Prioritize strong, clear visuals, concise captions, helpful stories/carousels, and short demonstrations. Include technical detail when it helps the audience understand the product and is verified. Do not present stock imagery or generated imagery as a photo of the actual product.

## 6. Facebook Guidance

Facebook posts may contain more context, product details, practical explanations, and approved announcements. Keep paragraphs readable and make the next step clear. Use only verified contact links and active offer terms.

## 7. TikTok Guidance

Short-form videos may begin with a useful question, relevant situation, or clear demonstration. Show the product accurately, avoid fabricated before/after results, and do not make every video a direct advertisement. Any specification shown on screen or in a caption must be supported by approved data.

## 8. Claim and Asset Rules

Never invent or imply:

- Product features, compatibility, performance, results, safety, or durability
- Prices, discounts, free shipping, offer deadlines, or inventory
- Reviews, customer names, testimonials, purchases, or user experiences
- Company history, partners, certifications, awards, or market ranking
- Delivery times, warranty coverage, or return eligibility

Use actual approved product images where available. If an asset is illustrative, label it where needed and never mislead viewers into thinking it is the exact product.

## 9. Calls to Action

Use only a real destination approved in file `06`, such as a published product page or verified business contact channel. Do not add fictional profile URLs or a WhatsApp number. Do not imply a purchase was completed if a click only opens a page or external application.

## 10. Pre-Publication Checklist

- [ ] Product and promotion details are current and verified.
- [ ] Every material claim has a reliable source or owner approval.
- [ ] Asset represents the actual product, or is clearly illustrative.
- [ ] No fake review, scarcity, urgency, or performance result is used.
- [ ] Arabic/English copy, when both are published, communicates equivalent facts.
- [ ] CTA points to a real, approved destination.
- [ ] Offer terms and validity are clear when an active offer is advertised.

**Rule:** Editorial creativity may vary. Business facts must not.
